# Marriage Feud

A ten-round Family Feud-style game for a marriage fellowship small group.

Open `index.html` in any browser — no build, no install, no internet needed after
the fonts load. It works offline, on a laptop, or projected.

## Playing

1. Enter the two team names, pick 5 or 10 rounds, and set the points multiplier.
2. Press **Start the game**.
3. The host reads the question. Teams call out answers.
4. Click a board tile when a team's answer is on the board — it lights up and that
   team scores the answer's survey percentage.
5. Press **Strike** (or `S`) when a team guesses wrong. Three strikes and the turn
   passes; the team taking over scores double for anything they claim.
6. Press **Next round** (or `N`) to move on.

## Surveying

The baked-in answers and percentages are a default survey. To use your own
group's real answers, press **Survey** on a round and type them in — the board
updates immediately.

## Controls

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `S` | Strike |
| `U` | Undo strike |
| `N` | Next round |
| `T` | Turn it over |
| `M` | Sound on/off |
| `1`–`8` | Reveal that answer slot |

Clicking a revealed answer's team buttons awards it to the other team instead.

## Ties

If the scores are level after the last round, a tiebreak round appears: both teams
write a guess, and the host picks the winner.

## Notes

- Game state is saved in the browser, so an accidental refresh won't lose the score.
- Works on a phone, a laptop, or a projector.
